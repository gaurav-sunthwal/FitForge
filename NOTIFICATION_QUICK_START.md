# 🎯 Quick Start: Notification System

## 🚀 What You Just Got

A complete notification system for:
- 💪 **Gym Selfie Reminders** - Daily reminder to track progress photos
- 🍽️ **Meal Tracking Reminders** - Breakfast, lunch, dinner tracking
- 💧 **Water Intake Reminders** - Stay hydrated throughout the day

## 📍 How to Access

1. Open your app
2. Go to **Profile** tab (bottom navigation)
3. Tap **Notifications** in the Settings section
4. Configure your preferences!

## ⚡ Quick Setup (3 Steps)

### Step 1: Grant Permission
When opening the notification settings for the first time, you'll be asked for notification permissions. **Tap "Allow"**

### Step 2: Customize Times
- **Gym Selfie**: Set to your post-workout time (e.g., 7 PM)
- **Breakfast**: Set to when you eat breakfast (e.g., 8 AM)
- **Lunch**: Set to your lunch time (e.g., 1 PM)
- **Dinner**: Set to your dinner time (e.g., 8 PM)
- **Water**: Choose how often (every 1-4 hours)

### Step 3: Test It
Tap the **"Send Test Notification"** button to verify it's working!

## 💡 Pro Tips

### For Best Results:

1. **Set Realistic Times**
   - Match your actual routine, not your ideal routine
   - You can always adjust later

2. **Start With Meal Reminders**
   - Most impactful for calorie tracking accuracy
   - The AI can analyze meal photos instantly!

3. **Use the AI Feature**
   - When you get a meal reminder → Take photo of your food
   - Go to Calories tab → Scan Food
   - AI automatically calculates nutrition! 🎉

4. **Gym Selfie Strategy**
   - Set notification for right after your typical workout
   - Creates a consistent before/after timeline
   - Visible progress = More motivation!

## 🔧 Troubleshooting

### Not receiving notifications?

**iOS:**
1. Settings → Notifications → FitForge → Allow Notifications

**Android:**
1. Settings → Apps → FitForge → Notifications → Enable

### Wrong times?
- Check your device timezone
- Verify times in notification settings
- Times use your local timezone
